How the apps connect

Admin → Client
KYC and bus approval, place requests, commission rules, settlement payments, suspension
Client → Admin
KYC documents, new buses, place requests, offer requests, settlement queries, bank changes
Client → Customer
Published trips and fares appear in search; delays and cancellations notify passengers
Client → Driver
Trip assignment appears in the driver app at once
Customer → Client and Admin
Bookings, payments, cancellations and refunds, SOS, support tickets
Admin → Customer
Banners, offers and coupons, refund status, support replies, SOS response
Driver → everyone
Boarding, check-ins, live location, delays, trip end, driver SOS

Try this end to end

  1. Customer app: sign in, search Bengaluru → Hyderabad for today, pick the Kaveri Express bus, choose seats and pay.
  2. Client portal (signed in as Kaveri Express): the booking appears in Bookings and in the bell.
  3. Driver app: sign in as the driver of that trip, start boarding, scan the ticket by PNR, start the trip.
  4. Customer app → My trips: the bus moves on the map. Press SOS.
  5. Admin app → SOS board: acknowledge it. The customer sees it at once.
  6. Client portal: accept an invitation, fill KYC; Admin app → KYC reviews: approve it, and the client portal unlocks.

Shared data

The data lives in this browser (localStorage) and starts fresh every day. Reset it here or from any app's demo controls.

Prototypes only: no real backend, payments or messages. The real apps are Flutter (customer, admin, client, driver) with a NestJS + MongoDB backend.
